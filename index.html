Clipspan

Loop any part of any video. Save it as an MP4.

Clipspan is a free Chrome extension that repeats the exact stretch you pick, on YouTube and most video sites, then records just that clip to MP4 in one click.

Dragging the scrubber back for the tenth time to catch one riff, one phrase or one move? Clipspan does the rewinding for you.

Three steps, no settings

  1. Open

    On any page with a video, press Alt+G or click the Clipspan icon.

  2. Pick the stretch

    Type a start and end time, like 1:23 and 1:45, and press Enter.

  3. Loop or record

    Press Loop to repeat it, or Record to save it as an MP4.

A looper and a recorder in one

Loop any stretch, on almost any site

A-B repeat on YouTube, course platforms, news sites and most pages with a standard web video player. Not just YouTube.

Record the video, not your screen

Clipspan captures the video and its sound straight from the player. No screen-share picker, no tabs or buttons in the shot, no cropping afterwards.

MP4 that plays everywhere

Clips save as MP4 (H.264 and AAC), ready for your phone, your editor or a social post. Or skip the times and just press Record now and Stop when you're done, even on a live stream.

Fast with the keyboard

Shortcuts work on any keyboard layout. On a Mac, use Option instead of Alt.

Your clips stay on your computer

Clipspan collects no data and sends nothing anywhere. Looping and recording happen inside your browser, and clips go straight to your Downloads folder.

Chrome will say Clipspan can "read and change data on websites". That is how it finds the video on the page you're watching. It doesn't read or send anything else.

Questions

Is Clipspan free?

Yes. Everything is free. If it saves you time, there's an optional tip jar on Ko-fi.

Which sites does it work on?

YouTube and most sites that use a standard web video player, including course platforms and news sites.

Can I record Netflix or other streaming services?

No. DRM-protected video can't be recorded. Only record videos you have the right to keep.

Does it work on live streams?

Yes. Leave the Start and End fields empty, press Record to capture what's playing right now, and press Stop to download the clip.

What do I need?

Chrome 126 or newer, which can record MP4 directly.

Is there a Mac or Windows difference?

It works the same in Chrome on Windows, Mac, Linux and ChromeOS. On a Mac, use Option instead of Alt: Option+G opens Clipspan, Option+L loops and Option+R records.

Does it slow down my browser?

Clipspan does nothing until you press Alt+G or click its icon.
